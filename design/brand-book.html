01 — Identity

The Mark

Grid Structure
4 × 5
A strict 20-node orthogonal grid. No diagonals, no curves. The letter S is resolved entirely within a rectangular matrix — encoding precision and constraint.
Node Geometry
Squircle
Each cell is 20 × 20 px with a border-radius of 8 px (40% of cell width). The squircle sits between a square's authority and a circle's approachability.
Active / Total
10 / 20
10 nodes active (Alert Orange). 10 inactive (Deep Neutral). The diagonal drift — top bar right-biased, bottom bar left-biased — encodes the S-curve within a pure orthogonal system.
Spacing
9 px gap
Uniform 9 px gutter between all cells on both axes. The gap-to-cell ratio of ~0.45 is intentional — enough air to read individual nodes, not so much that the grid dissolves.
Conceptual Metaphor
YOLO Grid
The matrix directly references YOLO (You Only Look Once) object detection grids — the same spatial framework Safion uses to partition video frames for real-time analysis.
Clear Space
1 cell
Minimum clear space on all sides equals one cell unit (20 px at native scale). Never crowd the mark. The surrounding negative space is part of the identity.
02 — Lockups

Logo Lockups

Primary Lockup — Dark Background
SAFION

Space Grotesk 500 · All-caps · Letter-spacing 0.30em. Mark scaled to 52 × 66 px. Gap between mark and wordmark: 20 px. This is the primary lockup for all dark-background applications.

Primary Lockup — Light Background
SAFION

On light backgrounds, inactive nodes shift to #D8D8E8 and the wordmark to #1A1A2C. Signal orange is unchanged — it must remain constant across all backgrounds.

Stacked Lockup — Square / App Icon Context
SAFION

Mark centered above wordmark. Wordmark reduced to 18 px with increased letter-spacing (0.42em) to match the visual width of the mark. Used for social profiles, app store icons, and square-format contexts.

Signal Orange — One job
Reserved exclusively for active detection nodes, alert states, CTAs, and the brand mark. Never use for decorative purposes. Its rarity is its power — when orange appears, something means something.
Void + Neutrals — The foundation
Three dark neutrals establish depth hierarchy. Void (#09090E) for canvas. Node Inactive (#181826) for the mark. Boundary (#1C1C2E) for structural rules. Never swap these — their subtle differences carry spatial information.
Frost — Not quite white
Pure white (#FFFFFF) is avoided. Frost (#EDEDF4) has a faint blue-violet undertone that harmonizes with the dark neutrals and prevents the sterile contrast of true white on near-black.

05 — Voice

Brand Voice

Precise
Speak in specifics. Never generalize.
Safion operates in environments where vagueness costs lives. Every piece of communication — from error messages to marketing copy — uses concrete, unambiguous language. "Flagged 3 PPE violations in Zone B" not "Detected some issues."
Authoritative
State facts. Don't hedge.
We don't say "might help you detect." We say "detects." Confidence is earned through accuracy, and our voice reflects that. Active voice always. Passive constructions signal uncertainty — and Safion is not uncertain.
Vigilant
Always-on, never anxious.
Vigilance isn't paranoia. We monitor continuously because that's how prevention works. Our tone is steady — never alarming for alarm's sake. We surface signal, not noise. An alert from Safion means something happened. Full stop.
Not This
Never alarmist, never vague, never casual.
Safion is not a startup chatbot. We don't use emoji in product copy. We don't say "Oops!" We don't use exclamation points. We don't say "leverage synergies." Safety is serious — our language reflects that gravity without becoming cold or robotic.
✓ Write Like This
"Stream 04 — hardhat violation detected at 14:32:07. Confidence: 97.4%. Operator notified."
✗ Not Like This
"Looks like we may have spotted something! A possible safety issue might have been detected in one of your cameras 🎥"
✓ Write Like This
"Safion processes 30 frames per second. It doesn't blink. It doesn't fatigue. It doesn't miss."
✗ Not Like This
"Our cutting-edge AI solution leverages next-gen computer vision to potentially help you maybe improve workplace safety outcomes!"
06 — Usage

Do & Don't

✓ Do
Use the mark on dark backgrounds with full contrast. Preserve the exact node states — no active/inactive inversions.
✗ Don't
Don't recolor the mark, change the border-radius to sharp corners, or alter the node pattern. Don't apply drop shadows or gradients.
✓ Do
SAFION
Use Space Grotesk 500, all-caps, letter-spacing 0.30em for the wordmark. Frost (#EDEDF4) on dark, Near-Black (#1A1A2C) on light.
✗ Don't
SAFION
Don't substitute the typeface, apply italic, use off-brand colors like purple, or change the case. The wordmark is not a design element — it's a legal mark.
